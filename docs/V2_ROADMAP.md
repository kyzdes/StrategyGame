# Corporate Empire v2.0 - "Empire Wars" 🚀

## Vision Statement

Transform Corporate Empire from a solid idle strategy game into a **viral, competitive, social phenomenon** that combines:
- **Deep Strategic Gameplay** - Tech trees, M&A, market manipulation
- **Competitive PvP** - Stock market wars, corporation battles, tournaments
- **Social Engagement** - Sharing, challenges, cooperative events
- **Rich Content** - Story campaigns, seasonal events, mini-games
- **Smart Automation** - AI advisors, predictive analytics, auto-optimization

---

## 🎯 Key Goals

1. **10x User Engagement** - From 10 min/day to 100 min/day average session
2. **Viral Growth** - Social sharing, referral system, competitive events
3. **Long-term Retention** - Seasonal content, story mode, endless progression
4. **Monetization** - Fair battle pass + cosmetics (no pay-to-win)
5. **Community Building** - Corporation wars, tournaments, global events
